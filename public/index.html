Emergency Priority Queue (Triage Inspector)

Max-Heap Logic

During emergencies, requests cannot be processed FIFO (First-In, First-Out). A Priority Queue ensures that CRITICAL incidents (e.g. cardiac arrest, active structural fire) are extracted first before MODERATE or LOW priority calls.
